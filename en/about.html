FAQ

Can I walk in without booking?

Yes, if a stylist is free. Booking is safer — it takes a minute on the site.

How long does colour last?

Single colour 4–6 weeks; balayage and AirTouch 3–4 months before a refresh.

Do you only work with women?

Yes, RoseLine is a women-only salon: cuts, colour, care and styling for women and girls.

How do I pay?

After the service — cash, card or transfer. No prepayment needed.

Can I come with a friend?

Yes. Mention it in the comment and we’ll book two stylists at the same time.
